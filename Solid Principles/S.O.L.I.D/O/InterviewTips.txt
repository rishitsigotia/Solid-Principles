1.Spotting Violations:

Look for classes with conditional logic (if/else, switch) that checks types/values to decide behavior.

Example: A PaymentProcessor with ProcessCreditCard(), ProcessPayPal() methods.

2.Defending Your Solution:

Say: "I used polymorphism/interfaces to decouple the behavior. New features are added by creating new classes, not changing existing ones."

Common Traps:

3.Myth: "OCP means you can never modify existing code."

Reality: Minimize changes to stable, tested code. Bug fixes are okay!

4.Buzzwords to Use:

"Abstraction over conditionals", "Strategy pattern", "Dependency Injection"
